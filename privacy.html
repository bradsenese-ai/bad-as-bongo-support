Bad As Bongo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Bad As Bongo is a bongo game made by Bradden Senese. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Camera

If you choose camera mode, the app uses your device's camera to track the position of your hands, arms and body so you can play the drums. This processing happens on your device in real time. The camera video is not shown, recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Information we collect

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or other personal information. Game settings such as volume levels are saved only on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email magnificentindicators@gmail.com.
