Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at magnificentindicators@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

The camera mode doesn't respond. Make sure Bad As Bongo has camera access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera, stand where your hands and arms are in view, and use good lighting.

I can't hear the drums. Check your device isn't on silent, turn the volume up, and check the Drums volume in the game's Settings.

How do I play without following a pattern? Choose Freestyle on the start screen.
